# Blockwild

A cooperative voxel sandbox for 1–10 players. Every player has a first-person view on their phone, tablet or computer; the host can play in first person on the same device or watch an island overview.

Survival is the default open-world sandbox: explore natural terrain, gather resources, build wherever you like, grow food, smelt ore, store supplies and establish a home. There are no quests, required steps or forced ending. Creative remains optional. See [the Survival rework](SURVIVAL.md) for mechanics, save compatibility and current world limits.

Desktop: click the world to capture the mouse, WASD to walk, Space to jump, left mouse to mine/hit and right mouse to build. Escape releases the mouse. Shift runs; in Creative, F toggles flight and Q descends. On touch, the left pad moves, dragging the world looks, and Mine/Build/Jump buttons perform actions. Creative adds Fly and Down. Craft opens recipes and the full material palette; Map shows terrain, explorer positions and recovery crates. Aim at clear grass or earth, then use Grow tree in Craft to renew your forest. It costs one berry and one earth in Survival and is free in Creative. The workbench also offers an explicit reload with low or balanced graphics.

The host's Room menu contains Save world, Load world and Finish session; results also offer Resume this world. The server autosaves every 30 seconds and before ending a world. The lobby offers Resume autosave and the previous world after everyone taps Ready. Downloads keep permanent copies; a sudden crash can lose changes since the last checkpoint. Saves preserve the world, inventories and recovery caches. Version-one files migrate into the expanded world. Inventories restore by player name. If names repeat, their original join order distinguishes the players; current saves also retain each recovery crate’s roster position. Use the same names and join order when resuming. Unmatched new players start with fresh supplies. Older saves with ambiguous cache ownership cannot identify which same-named player owned a crate.

Survival stacks hold 999 of each item. Crafting and mining stop before wasting a yield when its stack is full; a recovery crate keeps supplies that do not yet fit. A second death moves all earlier dropped supplies into the newest crate, without discarding them. The world allows 16,384 distinct changed blocks. Returning a cell to its original generated block releases that capacity. Changes travel as keyed deltas; reconnect gets a baseline. Craft/eat commands acknowledge once and do not consume the platform's per-round reliable-action IDs.

Run focused checks with `node --import tsx --test packages/games/blockwild/tests/*.test.ts`. Reproduce the maximum save-size/terrain geometry calculation with `node --import tsx output/blockwild/measure-budget.mjs`. The manager owns integrated immutable builds and server startup; do not rebuild a live run's assets.

Implementation: `model.ts` contains shared materials/recipes/types, `terrain.ts` and `legacy.ts` generate versioned worlds, `server.ts` owns simulation and validated saves, `commands.ts` handles optional pending-command storage, and `scene.tsx`/`meshes.ts` render the world. `client.tsx` composes shared controls with the game's HUD, map, workbench and results.

The current Survival rework passed634 combined tests, Chromium gameplay and maximum-load checks, and final WebKit compatibility checks. See [current QA](../../../output/blockwild/SURVIVAL-QA.md) for earned gameplay, controlled fixtures and remaining physical-device/human gates. The previous game-owned QA remains historical. No music, Git publication or external deployment is implied.
