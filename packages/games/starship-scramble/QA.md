## Limits and history

Physical iPhone Safari, Android touch, Wi-Fi contention, TV viewing distance, native browser chrome, and group enjoyment/pacing require human hardware testing. Browser emulation cannot establish these. The proposed 75–120 minute pacing target is not an observed human result; the catalog advertises 30 beacons and saving instead.
