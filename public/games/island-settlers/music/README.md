# Island Settlers soundtrack

Three MP3s supplied by the project owner on 13 September 2026 for background music in this game. Original audio and embedded metadata are preserved byte-for-byte; source filenames and SHA-256 hashes are in `manifest.json`. No separate license or artist attribution was provided; do not represent these as public-domain or independently licensed assets.

Rotation order:

1. Isle of Kingdoms (Board Game Soundtrack) — 3:12
2. Isle of Two Paths — 8:00
3. Knights, Robbers & Settlers - The Island Kingdom Suite — 8:37

About 19:49 per cycle. Only the host streams a single track at a time, at 22% media volume. End-of-track advances to the next entry and wraps to the first. Sound off pauses in place; Sound on resumes. Hidden pages, host disconnection, lobby/results and leaving the game stop playback. Phones and guest displays neither fetch nor play this playlist. Autoplay rejection waits for another user gesture; missing tracks are skipped, stopping after all three fail. Reconnection or a new user gesture can retry the exhausted playlist.
