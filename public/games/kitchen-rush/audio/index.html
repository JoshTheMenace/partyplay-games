KITCHEN RUSH

A little life in the kitchen.

Recorded kitchen sounds and bells. Try each clip below. The game plays them more quietly and limits overlapping effects. Music will come later.

Chop

Alternates with the second cut while someone chops.

Second cut

Adds a little variation to repeated chopping.

Cooking

A gentle bubbling loop while a stove or oven is cooking.

Stove starts

Gas catches when food starts cooking.

Food ready

A short bell when cooked food is ready to collect.

Burn warning

A stronger bell before burning, when burnt, and when fire starts.

Burning / fire

Crackling continues until the fire and burnt food are cleared.

Wash / extinguish

Water splashes while working, and when a fire goes out.

Dish clink

Plate handling and finished chopping or washing.

Order delivered

One bell for a successful delivery; two to open service.

Service ends

A short closing gong.
